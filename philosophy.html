Theoretical Base 01 / The Void

We strip away
the noise to discover
what remains.

Architecture is often misunderstood as the assembly of mass. We approach design from the inverse perspective. We treat physical materials as boundary markers whose sole purpose is to capture, frame, and define the volume of space between them. Our focus rests entirely on that empty space.

Three Core Pillars

The Manifest

Our architectural methodology rests on three unyielding constraints. We do not compromise on these protocols.

01

Structural Restraint

Every column, beam, and connection must justify its existence. If a structural element can be integrated or removed entirely without compromising the load path, it is deleted. We select local, honest materials: raw poured concrete, sandblasted Douglas fir, and structural carbon steel.

02

Light as Material

We do not treat light as an illumination source to be turned on or off. Natural sunlight is a critical building material. We choreograph deep light wells, clerestory openings, and calculated overhangs to direct shifting shadow patterns across surfaces, transforming static rooms throughout the daily cycle.

03

Absolute Essence

Reduction is not a sacrifice. It is the process of stripping away distractions until the remaining architecture becomes fully legible. By eliminating baseboards, door frames, decorative hardware, and applied finishes, we reveal the raw, unadorned dialogue between gravity and light.
